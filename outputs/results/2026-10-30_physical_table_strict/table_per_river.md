**Caption.** Physical characteristics of drought-to-flood transitions per river, pooled over the three locations per river, for Period 1 (1995–2009) / Period 2 (2010–2024). Droughts are runs of weeks with SRI < −1.0; floods are runs of weeks with weekly maximum runoff above the 69.1st percentile of the daily distribution of the same period. A drought is followed by a flood if a flood starts within 4 weeks after the drought ends. Obs/exp is the observed share of droughts followed by a flood divided by the share of all weeks that are followed by a flood start within the same window. Significant locations are those with Monte Carlo p < 0.05. Median lag is the number of weeks from drought end to flood start. Dominant season is the season in which most drought ends leading to a flood fall. Weeks with SRI < −1 is compared with the 15.9% expected for a well-fitted standardised index; much lower values indicate regulated flow. Δp is the median over the three locations of the difference in the probability of a flood peak exceeding the 0.69 runoff quantile after a severe versus a mild drought (drought-peak pair, bootstrap point estimate); per-location intervals are in per_location.csv.
